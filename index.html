☀️ Which UV affects you?

The UV Index measures the sunburn-producing UV radiation reaching the surface — it is not a direct reading of one UV type alone.

UV-A
  • Longer wavelength
  • Reaches Earth's surface in large amounts
  • Contributes to skin and eye exposure
  • Associated with premature skin ageing
UV-B
  • More strongly affected by the ozone layer
  • Major contributor to sunburn
  • Can damage skin and eyes
UV-C
  • Very harmful
  • Natural solar UV-C is essentially absorbed by the atmosphere
  • Normally does not reach Earth's surface
This dashboard does not measure separate UV-A, UV-B or UV-C values — no spectral sensor is connected. The UV Index shown reflects total sunburn-weighted UV reaching the ground, mainly UV-B with some UV-A contribution.

The ozone layer absorbs much of the Sun's harmful ultraviolet radiation, especially UV-B, and essentially all solar UV-C, before it reaches Earth's surface.
